### Asset Classification

Asset = a discrete continuity object.

Assets may be:
- embedded;
- attached;
- referenced;
- external;
- missing.

Guidance:

- Embedded: carried inside the current artifact.
- Attached: supplied alongside the current artifact.
- Referenced: identified but not supplied.
- External: exists outside the tape environment.
- Missing: known to matter but unavailable.

Recovery Manifest explains why important external assets matter, how they may be recovered, and what to do if recovery is not possible.

#### Purpose

Leverage compact semantic relationships to adjust **attention / visibility** of nodes with minimal token weight.

**Core principle:**  
Resonance scales **attention**. Decisions and evidence scale **authority** and **force**.  
Resonance is emphasis metadata, not mutation of truth or project standing.

#### Hard limits (non-negotiable)

205 **may** change:

- visibility / prominence / sort or attention scores  
- experimental linkage labels (A related-to B for this session)  
- workflow **flags** (see GATE)

205 **must not** change:

- truth or factual claims  
- evidence status  
- authority, approval, or ratification  
- epistemic status (e.g. speculative → observed/force)  
- project-force status (FORCE / “what still has force”)

Any real change to force, evidence, or authority uses normal pathways  
(Soft Depth / DESK / Continuity Anchors / explicit decision text) — **not** a RES token.

Decode ≠ verify. Notation ≠ decision.

#### Core protocol

1. **Token (trigger)**  
   Compact form, e.g. `[RES:PROMINENCE | TARGET:RISK-1 | PARAM:2]`

2. **Codebook**  
   Every token family used in an artifact should have recovery information stating:
   - **Target:** node kinds affected (e.g. RISK, QUESTION, ACTION)  
   - **Transformation:** allowed op (visibility / attention only, unless LINKER/GATE as below)  
   - **Logical dependency:** what must be present  

3. **Decoder**  
   Apply the codebook rule; do not “feel” resonance.  
   Report updated **attention/visibility** (or flag/link label), not a new truth status.

4. **Resonance State Update (RSU)** — **optional**, recommended for multi-hop or disputed use:

```yaml
resonance_state_update:
  trigger_token: "[RES:...]"
  target_node: "ID"
  attribute_changed: "visibility | attention_score | link_label | availability_flag"
  previous_state: "..."
  new_state: "..."
  calculation_logic: "..."
  dampening_applied: yes | no | not_applicable
```
Simple single prominence bumps do not require an RSU.

5. **Dampening (loop guard)

Do not re-apply resonance to the same node in the same session without an explicit dampening param (e.g. half factor). Prefer one pass.

6. **Delta (proof)

Success = successor prioritisation/orientation improves vs plain text without inventing force, evidence, or authority.
